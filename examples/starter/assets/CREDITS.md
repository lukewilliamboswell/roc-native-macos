# Credits

## The default song

`polymorphic-defunctionalization.mp3` and its timed lyrics,
`polymorphic-defunctionalization.lrc`, are *Polymorphic Defunctionalization
Through Lambda Set Specialization* by Luke Boswell, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY
4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). You may share and adapt
them, commercially too, as long as you credit Luke Boswell, link the
licence and say if you changed them.

The song is a prog-metal setting of the `twice` example from William
Brandon, Benjamin Driscoll, Frank Dai, Wilson Berkow and Mae Milano,
[Better Defunctionalization through Lambda Set
Specialization](https://doi.org/10.1145/3591260) (PLDI 2023), the research
behind how the Roc compiler compiles closures. Its lyrics, walkthrough,
arrangement and liner notes are at
<https://gist.github.com/lukewilliamboswell/be88b49154f602495f2821a01f995d1f>.

The app ships the gist's compact MP3 and timed LRC under the same basename.
To start with an empty queue, follow the instructions by `Store.default`
in `src/Store.roc`.
